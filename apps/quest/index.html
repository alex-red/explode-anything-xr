EXPLODE

Pull apart the headset you are wearing, on your own desk. Then do the same to a codebase.

  1. 1. About 90 seconds: a model of a headset stands on your desk. Pull it apart, cut its camera link, and watch your room go dark.
  2. 2. Then a login request, traced through a TypeScript service back to its source lines. Break it, see the recorded test failure, fix it.

Meta Quest 3 or 3S. Seated at a desk, hands only (controllers also work). About five minutes. Uses your headset's room setup to find the desk if you allow it; works without it.

The headset and the drivetrain are simplified teaching models, not the devices' actual internals. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta. Dimming your room is an illustration; nothing about your headset changes. auth-service is a sample service written for this demo. “Observed” means a test run recorded when the content was built; nothing runs live.
